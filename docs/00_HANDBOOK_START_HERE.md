# Handbook Start Here

## What is this application?
This is a complete School Management Application that manages the daily operations of a school, including students, staff, gate passes, fees, and more.

## Who uses it?
- **Admin**: Manages school, settings, fees, staff, and overall data.
- **Staff**: Teachers who manage classes, attendance, homework, and activity logs.
- **Parent**: Views child's progress, homework, announcements, and pays fees.
- **Gate Staff**: Manages student pick-ups via QR gate passes.
